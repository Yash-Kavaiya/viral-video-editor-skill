# Viral Video Patterns Reference

This reference describes research-backed patterns that make short-form videos go viral.
The `viral_scorer.py` script uses these patterns to score each video segment.

## Hook Patterns (First 3 Seconds)

| Pattern | Description | Example |
|---------|-------------|--------|
| Bold Statement | Controversial or surprising claim | "Everything you know about X is wrong" |
| Direct Question | Ask the viewer something relatable | "Have you ever wondered why..." |
| Mid-action Start | Begin clip at peak action moment | Jump cut to the best part |
| Number Hook | Quantified promise | "3 things I wish I knew before..." |
| Empathy Hook | "If you [relatable situation], watch this" | Speaks to specific audience |

## Viral Content Categories

1. **Educational** — "I didn't know that!" reaction
2. **Emotional** — heartwarming, funny, shocking
3. **Aspirational** — before/after, transformation
4. **Controversial** — challenges common beliefs
5. **Timely** — relates to trending topics

## Optimal Duration by Platform

| Platform | Sweet Spot | Max |
|----------|-----------|-----|
| Instagram Reels | 7–15s or 30–60s | 90s |
| YouTube Shorts | 45–60s | 60s |
| TikTok | 21–34s or 60s | 90s |

## Audio Virality Signals

- **Confident speech pace**: 130–160 words per minute
- **Dynamic range**: Mix of loud and soft moments
- **Trending audio**: Using platform-trending sounds increases reach 3–5x
- **No dead air**: Every second should have sound

## Visual Virality Signals

- **Face-forward camera**: Direct eye contact = 30% more engagement
- **High contrast**: Bold colors, clear subject/background separation
- **Motion**: Camera movement or subject movement signals energy
- **Text on screen**: 85% of videos watched without sound — captions are essential
- **First frame impact**: Thumbnail-worthy opening frame
